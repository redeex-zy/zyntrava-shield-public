# Licensing decision — qualified Apache-2.0 source grant

The creator has approved Apache-2.0 for original material they have authority to
license, with AI-assisted authorship and no known other human copyright contributor.
[The application record](license-application.md) preserves the exact scope and limits.
The earlier sealed candidate/audit remain unchanged; this is a new source snapshot.

Apache-2.0's permissive integration model and explicit patent provisions suit an
embeddable SDK. MIT was a simpler alternative without equivalent express patent
terms; AGPL would impose materially different copyleft obligations. The creator's
actual choice is Apache-2.0, not a choice inferred from old metadata.

Read the [official license](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0),
[local complete text](../LICENSE), [NOTICE](../NOTICE),
[scope/exclusions](license-scope.md) and [third-party notices](../THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).
The grant cannot override upstream copyrights. The Next.js-generated declaration
file retains MIT attribution; separately installed dependencies keep their own terms.
No broad ownership warranty is made about AI-generated output, facts or third parties.

The SDK wheel/sdist contain original package sources and existing LICENSE/NOTICE;
they exclude the dashboard template and its implementation dependencies. The full
source ZIP includes the template and its MIT notice. Future binary/model/compiled
distributions need a new component-level review, including applicable source,
attribution and relinking requirements. There is no trademark or legal clearance claim.
