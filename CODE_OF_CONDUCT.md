# Community conduct

Be respectful, technically honest and welcoming across nationalities, languages,
experience levels and identities. Critique ideas and evidence without harassment,
threats, discrimination or disclosure of private information. Obtain authorization
before testing a system and follow coordinated vulnerability disclosure.

This applies to project issues, reviews, discussions and project events once those
channels exist. The project creator is responsible for moderation; no separate
committee or external organization is claimed. Reports should be made privately
through a verified maintainer channel when configured. A public moderation contact
and escalation procedure must be established before opening community channels.
